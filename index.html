      Good to know

      • Give it time. Most formulas need around 8 to 12 weeks of consistent daily use before changes are likely to be noticeable.
      • Sunscreen comes first. Daily broad-spectrum SPF 30+ matters more than any capsule.
      • Check the label. Look for third-party testing and minimal fillers.
